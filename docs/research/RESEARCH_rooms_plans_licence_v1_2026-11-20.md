## The answer in one paragraph

The plans' pages give a buyer no right to derive anything from them, and no right to publish. Their footer says the content may not be used without permission in any form. Editing is sold separately, as a licence from $100 a deck ($1,000 for the set), agreed by email; vector and CAD files go by contract. Nothing on the pages speaks to tracing the plans into coordinates, or to a model built from them. So a table of rooms traced from these plans cannot go into this repository, which is public, nor onto its site, without the makers' written leave. There are two honest ways on: ask them for a licence that covers publishing derived room outlines (draft below, for Bo); or keep the table private, off the repository and never deployed, for Bo's own walks. Meanwhile, stages W0 (done) and R1 (the rooms reader at level 0, from the model alone) need no plan at all.

## What the pages say [R]

| Question | What the pages say |

| What a buyer may do | The product pages speak of "examination and study" (marketing wording, not a licence). The FAQ allows printing for one's own use and asks buyers to respect the makers' copyright wishes. Commercial use is not mentioned. |
| Derived works (tracing to a table of rooms, a 3D model), and publishing them | Not given to buyers. The licensing page sells editing rights in tiers. Tier One ("can be edited by the licensee") is $100 a deck or $1,000 for the set; it needs the Digital Plans Package and an agreement by email. Tiers Two and Three (PDF or vector, DWG) go by a licensing agreement or contract, at the creator's discretion. Tracing to coordinates, 3D models and publishing derived data in a repository, a site, a game or an app are not mentioned. |
| Passing on the image files | The footer forbids use "without permission in any form". The files come by a password-protected link. The makers do not share PDFs or the original CAD files. A purchase may be given as a gift. |
| Attribution, and asking leave | No attribution rule is stated. Permission is needed; the licensing page names the address to ask at. |
| Who holds the copyright | The footer names Vintage Digital Revival, LLC. No date is given for the terms. The plans carry their own version dates (4.50 in April 2026, 4.60 in September 2026). |

The summary of the product page rests partly on the fetch's own digest of it; the footer and the licensing page were read directly.

## What it means for the stages

- **R3 onward (tracing tiles) cannot start for the public repository** without written leave that covers publishing a table of room outlines and a wireframe generated from it. A Tier One licence ("can be edited by the licensee") may or may not cover publishing; it has to be asked.
- **The pictures** stay outside the repository in every case, as v1 and v1.1 said.
- **W0 and R1 are untouched by this:** they use the model's own numbers only.
