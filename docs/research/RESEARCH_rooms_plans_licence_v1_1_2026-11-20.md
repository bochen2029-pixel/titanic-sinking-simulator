## The decision, and its rules

The coordinator's rules, on Bo's word:

1. **The rooms' facts may go into the repository as a sourced table.** Which room, on which deck, in which watertight compartment, where, and how big. Bo holds that the 1912 originals are public domain, and facts are no one's.
2. **No modern redrawing's images or drawings go into the repository or onto its site.** That covers the free deck plans of Encyclopedia Titanica and the paid plans of the Titanic Historical Group. This morning's rewrite of main took two Encyclopedia Titanica overlay pictures out for exactly that reason.
3. **Encyclopedia Titanica's free deck plans are the reading reference: read on the page, not downloaded.** A session reads a room's place and size off the page and writes the numbers.
4. **Each row is checked against the 1912 originals where they can be reached,** such as the inquiries' exhibits or scans of the builder's general arrangement. Each row cites its source: the reference read, and the original it was checked against, or "not checked against an original".

## Practical notes for whoever reads the decks (R3 on)

- Read off the page with the browser: positions by her frames where the plan prints them, sizes in feet and inches where written. Never save the page's images.
- Snap a wall to its frame number wherever the plan gives one. That is where the inch accuracy comes from, as v1 said.
- Cite each row, for example: "ET deck plans, E deck, frame 45 to 52 port; checked: the inquiry's exhibit, E deck". Cite by page and deck, not by image file.
- The modern redrawings may differ from the 1912 originals, or from each other. Where they part, the row says so and the original wins.
