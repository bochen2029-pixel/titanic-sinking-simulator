## 0. Summary

- **The damage in boiler room 5 was in its forward starboard bunker, about 2 ft abaft the bulkhead, about 2 ft above
  the stokehold plates** (the British Report and Barrett, Q2105 [read]). The bunker ran 9 ft along the ship's side
  (the Solicitor-General's figure [read]).
- **It did not reach the stokehold's own side.** While Barrett was in boiler room 5 "the plates never got covered"
  (Q2109 [read]); the water in that room came from the bunker, whose door was shut (the Report [read]), until the rush
  at about 12:40 (the Report) or 1:10 (Barrett). A tear aft of the bunker would have flooded the stokehold directly.
  And "In No. 4 boiler room there was no indication of any damage" (the Report [read]).
- **How much: "as it would from an ordinary fire hose"** (the Report, from Barrett Q2255 [read]), less than into No. 6
  (Q2099 [read]). Barrett "could not estimate exactly how large the hole was" (Q2107), and the Commissioner said of his
  evidence on the point, "I should place very little reliance upon his evidence" [read].
- **Against a short end in boiler room 5:** the Report's "Extent of the Damage" gives its whole length as "about 300
  ft" [read]. From the forepeak's damage (about x 124 m) to 2 ft into boiler room 5 (x 53.6) is about 70 m, 231 ft [derived];
  300 ft aft of x 124 is x 33, in boiler room 4, which the same Report says was undamaged. The two statements cannot
  both be exact; the room-by-room account is the one built on a witness who stood there.
- **The model:** the 1912 damage preset puts its one opening in boiler room 5 at 0.7 m abaft the bulkhead, inside the
  bunker [measured]: as the record. The 1912 watch's ice opens boiler room 5 with 12 openings from 0.8 to 11.8 m abaft
  the bulkhead, 0.063 m2, of which 72 % (0.045 m2) lie aft of the bunker, in the stokehold's own side [measured]. The
  encounter's fit (e17) aimed the tear's aft end at x 53.2 m (1.05 m abaft the bulkhead) but weighed it against the
  area's split by compartment (Hackett and Bedford's), the sighting distance and the impact's timing; the point it
  chose ends at x 42.4 m.

## 2. Reading

- **The tear's aft end lay within the bunker's 9 ft (2.74 m) abaft the bulkhead,** most likely near its forward end
  where Barrett saw the water. Three facts hold it there: the place he saw it; the stokehold's plates dry until the rush,
  which a tear in the stokehold's own side would not allow; and no damage in boiler room 4. The Commissioner's caution
  widens the place within the bunker, not beyond it.
- **The "about 300 ft" is the weak figure.** It is a round length in a summary paragraph; taken from the forepeak it
  ends in boiler room 4, which the same Report finds undamaged [derived]. From the stem's forefoot (about x 128 m) to
  the bunker's after end (x 51.5 m) is 76.5 m, 251 ft [derived], the nearest the room-by-room account allows.
- **How much came into boiler room 5 is uncertain.** "An ordinary fire hose" of 1912 (2.5 in at fire-main pressure)
  passes some hundreds of gallons a minute [bet: not sourced here], about a tonne a minute; the model's one opening there
  on the preset (0.045 m2, Hackett and Bedford's 6 % of the area) admits about 20 tonnes a minute at the night's head
  [measured, HANDOFF 4c]. That is outside G6, which is about where, but it bears on how fast the bunker fills (W6).

## 3. The model, measured (wt/w6 at eedaf22; the encounter as on main 0f3f4d1)

- **The preset:** `TITANIC_SPLIT` in `core/scenarios.js` puts boiler room 5's share (6 %, after Hackett and Bedford)

- **The watch:** at the end of the 1912 encounter the ice has made 82 openings, 0.7506 m2. In boiler room 5: 12, 0.0629
  m2, at 0.8, 1.8, 2.8, 3.8, 4.8, 5.8, 6.8, 7.8, 8.8, 9.8, 10.8 and 11.8 m abaft the bulkhead (90, 85, 80, 74, 67, 59,
  51, 42, 35, 26, 16, 5 cm2); the first two (0.0175 m2) lie in the bunker, the other ten (0.0454 m2, 72 %) in the
  stokehold's own side. Boiler room 4 (aft of x 36.88) is not reached.
- **Why:** the 1912 geometry (`GEOM_1912` in `core/night.js`: the ice 257 m ahead, reaching 47 m to port, when the helm
  went over) was found by e17's search, whose score is the tear's extent against x 124 to 53.2 m, half the split's error
  against Hackett and Bedford's in points, the sighting's distance from 457 m over 5, and the seconds outside 20 to 25 s
  from the order to the impact. At the chosen point the tear runs x 123 to 42 (`core/night.js`, line 56: "tears x 123 to
  42, from the stem into boiler room 5; split error 29 points"): its after end misses the target by 11 m, traded for the
  other terms.
- **What it costs W6:** on the watch boiler room 5's stokehold floods by its own side from the first minutes, the head
  across the bunker's wall never builds as on the preset, and the bunker cannot hold the water off. W6's early list
  moves the right way on the preset and barely on the watch (W6 v1.1, section 3).

   the bunker.
