# The User Guide

- **Edit a chapter:** the chapters are `src/NN-name.md`, read in the order of their numbers; `src/guide.json` holds the edition, its date, the output's name and the page and size budgets. A new edition is a new `edition`, `date` and `file` there, and the last edition's built files are taken out (the hand-written first edition, `USER_GUIDE_v1_2026-11-17`, stays).
- **The dialect:** Markdown with `{{Label}}` for a control by its label on the screen, `{{:Label}}` for a heading or readout by its words, `[[A]]` for a key, `@@fact@@` for a number or sentence pulled from the repository (`@@ch.name@@` is a chapter's number), `:::gen name` blocks for the generated tables, `:::note`, `:::tip` and `:::lede` boxes, and `![alt](img/x.jpg "caption"){.side}` figures (`.side`, `.half`, `.stage`, `.phone`; image lines in one paragraph make a row). The facts and blocks are listed in `tools/build_guide.js` (FACTS, GEN).
- **Retake the pictures** after the site changes: `node tools/build_site.js`, then `node tools/guide_shots.js` (about a minute; `--only watch,pumps,stem,preset,damage,phone` for some scenes, `--verify` to retake and compare byte for byte). It writes `img/` and `shots.json`: the pictures, and what the page said and held on the 1912 watch (its sentences, the boats, the wireless, the reports, the card) and the hull gauge's curve on the 1912 damage.
- **Rebuild:** `node tools/build_guide.js` writes the HTML and prints the PDF with headless Edge or Chrome (about 10 s); `--html` skips the PDF.
- **The Carpathia question** (chapter 6) quotes the first search's result: `out/carpathia_best.json` and its note, `docs/research/RESULT_carpathia_search_v2_2026-11-20.md` (`CARPATHIA` in `tools/build_guide.js`; a later note is named there). When both are in the tree every figure is pulled from them; when they are not, the build says so, quotes `CARPATHIA_TYPED`, and records them as absent, so that the check calls the guide stale once they arrive.
- **Check:** `node tools/guide_check.js` fails when a named control has left the page, a picture is missing or unused, a figure or table does not fit the page, or the guide is older than its sources; `--pages DIR` saves every PDF page as a PNG to look at.
- **Look at the pages** after a change: the check's `--pages` folder shows them as a reader sees them.
- Templates: `template/guide.html` (cover, contents) and `template/guide.css` (one stylesheet for the screen and for Letter paper).
